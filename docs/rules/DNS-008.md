# DNS-008: SPF record observed with more than 10 DNS lookups

- **Area:** dns
- **Severity:** low

## What is observed

The SPF record has more than 10 mechanisms that need a DNS lookup. Included records are not followed.

## Why it can matter

The SPF standard allows at most 10 mechanisms that need a DNS lookup while a record is evaluated. The terms include, a, mx, ptr, exists and redirect each count. A record that goes over the limit is a permanent error, and receivers may fail SPF checks for the domain. A long list of included senders reaches the limit quickly.

## False positives

Sentinel counts only the terms written in the record it fetched. It does not follow include or redirect targets, so the real total can be higher than the number it reports, and a record under the limit here can still go over it once the included records are expanded. Some receivers apply a different limit. The lookups of a, mx and include are counted together, but nested ones are not counted here.

## How to fix

### DNS zone

```
; Replace includes of senders you no longer use, and where a sender has
; fixed addresses, list them directly. ip4 and ip6 need no lookup.
example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=spf1 ip4:192.0.2.0/24 ip6:2001:db8::/32 include:_spf.example.net ~all"
```
