# DNS-010: DMARC record observed with a partial pct

- **Area:** dns
- **Severity:** low

## What is observed

The DMARC record uses p=quarantine or p=reject with a pct below 100, so the policy applies to only part of the mail.

## Why it can matter

The pct tag sets the share of failing mail that the DMARC policy applies to. With p=quarantine or p=reject and a pct below 100, the rest of the failing mail is handled as if the policy were one step weaker. The tag is meant for a gradual rollout, so a value below 100 that stays in place leaves part of the mail outside the policy.

## False positives

A partial pct is normal while a policy is being rolled out, and some domains keep it for a while on purpose. Sentinel does not judge pct when p=none, because pct changes nothing there and DNS-005 already covers that policy. It reads one DNS answer and cannot tell how long the value has been in place.

## How to fix

### DNS zone

```
; Once the reports look clean, apply the policy to all mail.
; pct=100 is the default, so the tag can also be removed.
_dmarc.example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@example.com"
```
