# DNS-009: DMARC record observed without a reporting address

- **Area:** dns
- **Severity:** informational

## What is observed

The DMARC record has no rua tag, so no address receives aggregate reports.

## Why it can matter

The rua tag in a DMARC record names an address that receives aggregate reports. The reports list which servers sent mail for the domain and whether it passed the SPF and DKIM checks. Without them, the domain owner has no view of legitimate mail that would fail a stricter policy, or of mail from servers they do not know about.

## False positives

Some domains leave reports off on purpose, for example when they use a reporting service that publishes the address elsewhere. Sentinel reads the first rua tag in the record it found, and it cannot tell whether reports arrive some other way. It checks the record at the scanned name, or at the organizational domain when the scanned name has none. This is informational for that reason.

## How to fix

### DNS zone

```
; Add a rua tag. Aggregate reports are sent to this address.
_dmarc.example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@example.com"
```
