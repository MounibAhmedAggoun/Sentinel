# DNS-002: DMARC record not observed

- **Area:** dns
- **Severity:** low

## What is observed

No DMARC record was seen for the scanned name.

## Why it can matter

DMARC is a DNS record that tells receivers what to do with mail that fails SPF or DKIM checks, and where to send reports about it. Without one, receivers apply their own handling, and the domain owner gets no reports.

## False positives

Sentinel looks for a DMARC record at _dmarc.&lt;name&gt; and then at the organizational domain, so a subdomain that inherits the policy is not reported. A name that sends no mail may not need one. Sentinel skips this rule when the DNS server did not clearly answer, and for IP address targets.

## How to fix

### DNS zone

```
; Start with p=none to collect reports before enforcing anything.
_dmarc.example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@example.com"
```
