# TLS-011: Certificate validity period observed above the CA/Browser Forum maximum

- **Area:** tls
- **Severity:** informational

## What is observed

The certificate was issued for longer than the maximum that applied on its issue date.

## Why it can matter

Publicly trusted certificate authorities follow the CA/Browser Forum Baseline Requirements, which cap how long a certificate may be valid. For certificates issued on or after 2026-03-15 the cap is 200 days, from 2027-03-15 it is 100 days, and from 2029-03-15 it is 47 days. Shorter lifetimes limit how long a stolen key or a wrong certificate stays usable. A certificate valid for longer than the cap on its issue date is unusual for a public CA.

## False positives

The cap does not apply to private or internal certificate authorities, which can issue longer certificates. Sentinel skips self-signed certificates and certificates the system trust store did not accept, but a private CA that happens to be trusted on the scanning machine can still be reported. Certificates issued before 2026-03-15 are not checked, because their cap was 398 days. Sentinel reads the dates from the leaf certificate only.

## How to fix

### certbot

```
# Public CAs issue short-lived certificates, so renew automatically.
certbot renew --dry-run
```
