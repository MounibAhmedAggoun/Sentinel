# DNS-007: More than one SPF record observed

- **Area:** dns
- **Severity:** low

## What is observed

The TXT records of the scanned name contain more than one SPF record.

## Why it can matter

A domain may publish only one SPF record. When the TXT records of a name contain two or more, the SPF standard treats it as a permanent error, and receivers may fail every SPF check for that domain, including mail from senders that were meant to be allowed. It often happens when a second service asks for its own record and it is added next to the first one.

## False positives

Sentinel counts TXT values that start with v=spf1 at the scanned name. A TXT record split into several strings is joined by the resolver, so it counts as one. The records are read from one lookup, and a stale answer from a resolver cache can show a record that was already removed. Receivers differ in how they handle the error.

## How to fix

### DNS zone

```
; Two records at the same name, which is the error:
;   example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=spf1 include:_spf.example.net ~all"
;   example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=spf1 include:mail.example.org ~all"
; Replace them with one record that combines the mechanisms:
example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=spf1 include:_spf.example.net include:mail.example.org ~all"
```
