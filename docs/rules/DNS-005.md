# DNS-005: DMARC record observed with a monitoring-only policy

- **Area:** dns
- **Severity:** informational

## What is observed

The DMARC policy is p=none, which reports failures but asks receivers to take no action.

## Why it can matter

A DMARC policy of p=none asks receivers to take no action on mail that fails the checks, and only to send reports. It is a common first step, because the reports show which mail would be affected before anything is enforced.

## False positives

This is an expected state while DMARC is being rolled out, which is why it is informational. Some domains keep p=none on purpose. Sentinel cannot see whether reports are being read or whether a stricter policy is planned.

## How to fix

### DNS zone

```
; After the reports look clean, move to quarantine, then to reject.
_dmarc.example.com.  IN  TXT  "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@example.com"
```
